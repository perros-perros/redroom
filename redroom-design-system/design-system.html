(Design System) - 02 Built on Tailwind's 4px grid Primary · #7B1F2B

A system in burgundy.
Composed on the 4-px grid.

Every spacing, sizing, and gap utility flows from Tailwind's base unit of 0.25rem - 4 pixels at the default browser size. Burgundy and Montserrat sit on top as a custom theme.

(Spacing) - 06 - Tailwind grid
